# 🎯 Interview Prep

You have built the fundamentals — now let's put them to work. This section is
**practice material for job interviews**, written for people who have finished
the curriculum (or are finishing it). It does not teach new concepts. It pulls
together what the site already taught and asks you to perform with it.

Two ground rules before you start:

1. **Answer out loud first.** Interviews are spoken. Try every question with
   your voice before you peek at the answer — it feels awkward, and it works.
2. **Every miss is a signpost.** If a question stumps you, follow its link back
   to the module, re-read that one section, and try again tomorrow. That loop
   is the prep.

## 📅 A four-week prep plan

Steady beats cramming. Roughly an hour a day for four weeks gets you through
everything here twice — once to learn, once to sharpen.

| Week | Focus | Files |
|------|-------|-------|
| 1 | Language fundamentals: mutability, references, scope, functions | [python_questions.md](python_questions.md) (Basics & Mutability, Data Structures, Functions & Scope) + the matching [quizzes](../quizzes.md) |
| 2 | Objects, iteration, errors: OOP, generators, exceptions | [python_questions.md](python_questions.md) (OOP, Iterators & Generators, Exceptions & Context Managers) |
| 3 | Under the hood: concurrency, stdlib, then problems on paper | [python_questions.md](python_questions.md) (Concurrency, Stdlib Gems) + [algorithm_problems.md](algorithm_problems.md) (start with the easy tier) |
| 4 | Full run-throughs: design, behavior, mock interviews | [system_design_basics.md](system_design_basics.md), [behavioral_tips.md](behavioral_tips.md), then re-attempt every question you missed in weeks 1–3 |

A few tips that make the plan work:

- **Write the code, don't just read it.** For every "what does this print?"
  question, run the snippet in a real interpreter and change one thing.
- **Explain your reasoning while you solve.** Interviewers grade the path, not
  just the destination — the algorithm problems file practices exactly this.
- **Keep a miss list.** One file, question numbers only. Week 4 is that file.

## 🎤 Running a mock interview alone

You do not need a partner to practice performing:

1. Pick five random questions from [python_questions.md](python_questions.md).
2. Set a timer for three minutes per question and answer **out loud**, in full
   sentences, as if a stranger is in the room.
3. Open the answer. Score yourself honestly: *clean*, *rough*, or *blank*.
4. For every *rough* or *blank*, follow the deep-dive link under the answer
   and add the question number to your miss list.

Twenty minutes, five questions, once a day — that is the whole system. By
week 4 your miss list will be shorter than you expect.
